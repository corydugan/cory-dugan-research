Dr Cory Dugan

Iron and women's health research

I am a PhD health scientist working at the intersection of women's health, iron metabolism, and health data. My research is published, and cited in World Health Organization guidance on patient blood management. I now build the screening and analysis tools that put that evidence to work, and I am extending that into health data and applied AI.

Evidence

Work published in JAMA Network Open, Anaesthesia, the British Journal of Sports Medicine and the British Journal of Haematology. The World Health Organization cites it in Guidance on implementing patient blood management to improve global blood health status (2024). Every tool I build is grounded in this body of work.

  • IRONWOMAN randomised controlled trial of intravenous iron therapy in iron-deficient recreationally active women. British Journal of Sports Medicine, 2025. Lead author.
  • Iron optimisation in pregnancy, a Haematology in Obstetric and Women's Health Collaborative consensus statement. Internal Medicine Journal, 2025. Co-author.
  • Systematic review of intravenous iron in non-anaemic iron deficiency, the work that guidance cites.

Breaking the Silence on Women's Health

A recorded Cassyni seminar, presented with Darsiha Balakirishnan, Fanija Dukovski, Hayley Caratti, Heather Bytheway and Talat Uppal.
